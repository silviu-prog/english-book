# Translation Brief — "Yeshua: Jesus Without Christianity" by Silviu Rotariu

You are translating a serious non-fiction / theological-philosophical book from
**Romanian into publishable literary English (US spelling)**. The English must read
as if originally written by a thoughtful English-language author — fluent, dignified,
reflective. Never word-for-word or machine-stilted. Faithful to meaning, tone, and
emphasis. **Do not summarize, abridge, or omit anything.** Translate the full text.

## Core terminology (MUST be consistent)
- **Yeshua** — keep as "Yeshua" everywhere. This is the book's deliberate choice
  (the historical Galilean) and must NOT be replaced with "Jesus", except where the
  author explicitly contrasts the two (e.g. "Iisus" used for the Christianized figure).
  Follow the author's own distinction: where the source says *Yeshua* → "Yeshua";
  where it says *Iisus/Isus* in contrast → "Jesus".
- **creștinism** → Christianity; **creștin** → Christian
- **Pavel** → Paul; **Moise** → Moses; **Isaia** → Isaiah; **Petru** → Peter;
  **Ioan** → John; **Iacov** → James; **Avraam** → Abraham; **Duhul Sfânt** → the Holy Spirit
- **Împărăția cerurilor / lui Dumnezeu** → the Kingdom of Heaven / of God
- **mântuire** → salvation; **pocăință** → repentance; **har** → grace;
  **ucenicie** → discipleship; **ucenic** → disciple; **păcat** → sin;
  **firea păcătoasă** → the sinful nature; **conștiință** → consciousness / conscience
  (choose by context: inner awareness = "consciousness"; moral faculty = "conscience")

## Bible quotations

## Formatting (output GitHub-flavored Markdown)
- Chapter title as a single `#` heading (use the English title given in your task).
- Each subsection title as a `##` heading. Subsection titles appear in the source as
  short standalone lines; translate them faithfully into English.
- Normal prose in paragraphs separated by blank lines.
- Convert Romanian guillemets/low quotes „ … " to English double quotes "…".
- Use em dashes (—) where the source uses them.
- Emphasis: render emphasized words in *italics* where the author clearly emphasizes.

The source text was extracted from a PDF and contains artifacts you MUST silently remove:
- **Standalone page numbers** (lines that are just digits).

  keep the title ONLY as the real heading, delete the repetitions.

- Reflow hard-wrapped lines into proper paragraphs.

Do not invent content. Do not add translator's notes. Output only the translated Markdown.
